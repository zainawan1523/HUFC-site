Season 02 · Regulations

Tournament Rules

Format, match regulations and code of conduct for all participating teams.
01 Format

Group Stage

All 5 teams play each other once in a single round-robin group stage, for a total of 4 matches per team (10 matches overall).

Knockouts

The top 4 teams on the points table advance to the semi-finals. Semi-final winners meet in the final; losers do not play a third-place match.

02 Squad Registration

Squad Size

Each team registers a minimum of 9 and a maximum of 12 players before the season opener. No player may be registered for more than one team.

Transfers

Player registrations lock once the group stage begins. No mid-season transfers are permitted.

03 Match Rules

Duration

Matches are played in two halves of 25 minutes each, with a 5 minute half-time break.

Substitutions

Each team may make up to 5 substitutions per match. Substituted players may return to the pitch.

Squad on Matchday

A team must field at least 7 players to start a match. Matches are forfeited 3–0 if a team cannot field the minimum.

04 Scoring System

Points

  • Win — 3 points
  • Draw — 1 point
  • Loss — 0 points

Tie-Breakers

Teams level on points are separated in this order: (1) head-to-head result, (2) goal difference, (3) goals scored, (4) toss.

05 Discipline & Conduct

Cards

Two yellow cards in the same match result in an automatic red. Any player receiving a red card sits out the team's next match.

Fair Play

Abuse of match officials, other players, or spectators will result in suspension for the remainder of the season, at the discretion of the tournament committee.
